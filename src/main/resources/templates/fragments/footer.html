Frequently Asked Questions

Navigate to the "Enroll" section from the menu. Browse available course offerings, and click "Enroll" on the courses you want to register for. You'll see confirmation once enrollment is successful.

Click on "Timetable" in the navigation menu. Your weekly schedule will display all enrolled courses with timings, rooms, and faculty information.

Notifications alert you about important events, deadlines, schedule changes, and announcements. Check the bell icon in the header to view unread notifications.
